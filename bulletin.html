FOOD FOR SOUL: IT IT IS ALREADY DONE

TEXT: Luke 13:12 “And when Jesus saw her, He called her to Him, and said unto her, Woman, thou art loosed from thine infirmity” Luke 13:12 (KJV).

Beloved, during the earthly ministry of Jesus, one day, He went into the temple and saw a woman who has been bowed over for 18 years. In other words, she was coming to church, yet nothing, no healing, and no deliverance because no one told her that she could be straightened. Religion says you can’t help yourself and this is how you must remain. Child of God, yes you cannot help yourself, but that does not mean you must remain like that. Jesus has paid the price for you; you no longer have to remain in the same situation. This woman was bound for eighteen years! And here comes Jesus, who knows how to give dignity to man. He said to her, Woman, thou art loosed from thy infirmity. He didn’t say, “you will be loosed”. In other words, woman, by covenant, you are loosed! But because you don’t know it, that is why you are still bowing. Today, I have come to declare that you are loosed from your infirmity. No matter what it is that has bowed you down, you are loosed. Just like the devil cannot stand before Christ, that mountain cannot stand before you. Who art thou o mountain that stands before Zerubbabel? It shall come down by the shout of grace, grace! Not by might, not by power but by the Spirit of the Lord. Happy Sunday to you all.
