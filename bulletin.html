FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: CALL TO REPENTANCE

TEXT : 2 PETER 3:9

Scriptures clearly teaches that it is God's desire for you to be saved. Salvation is the process where a rebellious and sinful man is ransomed and redeemed by the blood of the Lord Jesus Christ. Salvation comes when a man first of all realizes his position that without Christ, he is an enemy with God. Salvation comes when a man sees God's provision John 1:29 --Jesus the lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. A man may know many things but until he knows the gospel of Christ and receives God's gift by faith he is lost. The only way man can ever be redeemed is in the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Divine judgement is replaced with divne forgiveness. Need for mercy is replaced with a gift of grace. The unregenerate soul has now past from death unto life.
Happy Sunday to you all.
