FOOD FOR SOUL: SHINE YOUR LIGHT FOR OTHERS TO SEE

TEXT: Mathew 5:16 “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.” Matthew 5:16.

Beloved. Know who you are in Christ because if you fail to know who you are, the enemies will call you what you are not. The bible says you’re the light of the world (Mathew 5:14). Beloved, at conversion, you’ve received a mandatory call of God upon your life to tell others about Christ. Evangelism is a great commission; it is a compulsory assignment for all Christians. You were saved to save others. You can evangelise anywhere at any time as long as you are willing and have received the baptism of the Holy Spirit. One of the greatest evangelical methods is our lifestyle. Lifestyle evangelism simply means ‘live right and people will see the gospel in you, and when you live right, people will ask about your God. Lifestyle evangelism is best summed up in a phrase I’ve heard many times: “My life is my witness” (Act 1:8). Does your life witness Christ? As a child of God, is your life pointing others to Christ? Can you be referred to as Christian in the house where you live, in your environment, in your place of work and even in your family? The disciples were called ‘Christians’ in Antioch because people saw Jesus in their behaviour (Act 11:26). Always remember that you were saved to save others. Happy Sunday.
