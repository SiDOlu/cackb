FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: God will Fight for You

TEXT : Exodus 14 : 14

“The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.” Exodus 14:14 (KJV)
Beloved, though God’s people had left Egypt, Pharaoh still assembled all his military personnel and went after them to destroy them.
But God fought for them. You must settle one thing once and for all in your spirit: that having given your life to the Lord, you now belong to Him, and you have become His priority.
“He who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, how shall He not with Him also freely give us all things?" Romans 8:32 (NKJV).
God will freely give us all things because we are His priority. No matter where you find yourself in life, you matter to Him. According to the scriptures: “…. the very hairs of your head are all numbered.” Matthew 10:30 (NKJV).
The Lord will fight your battles. All you must do is sit down and be a spectator.
No matter what you are facing, you will not go back to Egypt. You will not serve the devil.
You won’t end in shame. Your life will end in glory! God is with you. He is already working everything out for your good. Happy Sunday to you all.
Happy Sunday to you all.

SUMMARY OF LAST WEEK MESSAGE BY: PASTOR R.R. FAJIMI

TOPIC: DIVINE FAVOUR

TEXT: LUKE 5 : 1 - 11

One day as Jesus was standing by the Lake of Gennesaret,[a] the people were crowding around him and listening to the word of God.
2 He saw at the water’s edge two boats, left there by the fishermen, who were washing their nets.
3 He got into one of the boats, the one belonging to Simon, and asked him to put out a little from shore. Then he sat down and taught the people from the boat.
4 When he had finished speaking, he said to Simon, “Put out into deep water, and let down the nets for a catch.”
5 Simon answered, “Master, we’ve worked hard all night and haven’t caught anything. But because you say so, I will let down the nets.”
6 When they had done so, they caught such a large number of fish that their nets began to break.
7 So they signaled their partners in the other boat to come and help them, and they came and filled both boats so full that they began to sink.
8 When Simon Peter saw this, he fell at Jesus’ knees and said, “Go away from me, Lord; I am a sinful man!”
9 For he and all his companions were astonished at the catch of fish they had taken,
10 and so were James and John, the sons of Zebedee, Simon’s partners. Then Jesus said to Simon, “Don’t be afraid; from now on you will fish for people.”
11 So they pulled their boats up on shore, left everything and followed him.
