FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: Be Excited/p>

TEXT: Psalm 122:1

I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the LORD. Beloved, the world needs Christians who are excited about what God can do and what God is doing. We have every reason to be excited. Just that you are excited with God does not mean you will not face battles. Attacks will come against you, hard battles will stand in front of you, but you can face those battles and those bad days with attitude of excitement for God because you know the ultimate. And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose. Rom.8:28. When you are excited it spreads and grows in the lives of everyone you come into contact with. Rejoice for Jesus is Lord..
