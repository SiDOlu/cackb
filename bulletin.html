FOOD FOR SOUL: God will Fight for You

TEXT: Exodus 14:14. “The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.” Exodus 14:14 (KJV)

Beloved, though God’s people had left Egypt, Pharaoh still assembled all his military personnel and went after them to destroy them. But God fought for them. You must settle one thing once and for all in your spirit: that having given your life to the Lord, you now belong to Him, and you have become His priority. “He who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, how shall He not with Him also freely give us all things?" Romans 8:32 (NKJV). God will freely give us all things because we are His priority. No matter where you find yourself in life, you matter to Him. According to the scriptures: “…. the very hairs of your head are all numbered.” Matthew 10:30 (NKJV). The Lord will fight your battles. All you must do is sit down and be a spectator. No matter what you are facing, you will not go back to Egypt. You will not serve the devil. You won’t end in shame. Your life will end in glory! God is with you. He is already working everything out for your good. Happy Sunday to you all.
