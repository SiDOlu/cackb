FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: Live for Christ

TEXT : 2 Timothy 3 : 12

“Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution.” 2 Timothy 3:12. Beloved, Paul here was telling us about the things he encountered; shipwreck, beaten several times, stoned and left for dead. He said all these things I endured for the sake of the gospel and out of them all, the Lord delivered me. You see there is nothing you have suffered for the kingdom’s sake, that the Lord will not bring deliverance to you.
It is guaranteed! You will be delivered! With that understanding, you can stand. All that will live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution; that is what we don’t want. Persecution is the price you pay for integrity. Any time you decide to live for God, you will be attacked; from the back, center left and right. It is going to come in diverse ways. You will be misunderstood, misconstrued, condemned, ridiculed and disparaged.
Societies are filled with the activities of the devil because the church is not ready to live for God. We are not ready to pay the price for integrity. We are the light of the world and the salt of the earth, but our light is not shinning. It is not shinning because we want to please the world. We want to be like others. We don’t want to be seen as odd people. We don’t want to pay any price.
Any time you commit to a life of integrity, get ready to walk a lonely road because friends and loved ones are going to desert you; not out of hatred, but out of weariness. They are tired and cannot continue with the journey. Be ready to walk alone. Be ready to be misunderstood, accused and called names. So, if you must be an agent of change, be willing to suffer. Be willing to pay some price. But the good thing is, as you trust God your faith will not fail.
Happy Sunday to you all.

TOPIC:GOD THAT DO HARD THINGS

TEXT: DANIEL 3: 14-30

14 and Nebuchadnezzar said to them, “Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, that you do not serve my gods or worship the image of gold I have set up?
15 Now when you hear the sound of the horn, flute, zither, lyre, harp, pipe and all kinds of music, if you are ready to fall down and worship the image I made, very good. But if you do not worship it, you will be thrown immediately into a blazing furnace. Then what god will be able to rescue you from my hand?”
16 Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego replied to him, “King Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to defend ourselves before you in this matter.
17 If we are thrown into the blazing furnace, the God we serve is able to deliver us from it, and he will deliver us[a] from Your Majesty’s hand.
18 But even if he does not, we want you to know, Your Majesty, that we will not serve your gods or worship the image of gold you have set up.”
19 Then Nebuchadnezzar was furious with Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, and his attitude toward them changed. He ordered the furnace heated seven times hotter than usual
20 and commanded some of the strongest soldiers in his army to tie up Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego and throw them into the blazing furnace.
21 So these men, wearing their robes, trousers, turbans and other clothes, were bound and thrown into the blazing furnace.
22 The king’s command was so urgent and the furnace so hot that the flames of the fire killed the soldiers who took up Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego,
23 and these three men, firmly tied, fell into the blazing furnace.
24 Then King Nebuchadnezzar leaped to his feet in amazement and asked his advisers, “Weren’t there three men that we tied up and threw into the fire?”
