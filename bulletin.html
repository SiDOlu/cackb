FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: Keep Standing

TEXT: Exodus 12:41

“And it came to pass at the end of the four hundred and thirty years, even the selfsame day it came to pass, that all the hosts of the LORD went out from the land of Egypt.” Exodus 12:41 KJV Pharaoh resisted Moses and the people of God. But they stood their ground. Many miracles were performed in Egypt but Pharaoh kept resisting. The devil is an oppressor! He does not give up easily. That is what oppressors do. They keep giving you an impression that they have the power of life and death. But you have a strong conviction, you will continue to stand. Hence the Bible declares: "...by knowledge shall the just be delivered" (Proverbs 11:9 KJV). We need understanding to know that what God has done is permanent. God is a covenant keeper and regardless of what the enemy is doing, God's promises can never fail. The people of God were in bondage for 430 years! To an ordinary mind, it was over - the promise and assurance of God could never come to pass. Pharaoh had taken over and God’s people would be slaves permanently. But God showed up supernaturally! The captivity of 430 years eventually came to an end. You will not be a slave to the devil! Demons will not dictate in your lives! You are about to enter what God has ordained for you. For too long, the devil has threatened your life but the God that we serve cannot lie!
There shall be an overnight miracle in your life. The Lord is bringing you out of every captivity.  Keep standing on the promises of God. You will not be ridiculed. You shall be massively blessed. Every device of the crafty against you shall be disappointed. Your rights and privileges will not be violently taken from you. God still rules in the affairs of men. You will not see shame! You will be settled. You shall never be stranded. So, I encourage you never to give up. Keep standing! Hold on tenaciously to the Word of God. He will show up for you.

TOPIC: Go AND Do LIKEWISE

TEXT: LUKE 10:25-37

34 On one occasion an expert in the law stood up to test Jesus. “Teacher,” he asked, “what must I do to inherit eternal life?” 26  “What is written in the Law?” he replied. “How do you read it?” 27  He answered, “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength and with all your mind’ [a ; and, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ [ 28  “You have answered correctly,” Jesus replied. “Do this and you will live.” 29  But he wanted to justify himself, so he asked Jesus, “And who is my neighbor?” 30  In reply Jesus said: “A man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho, when he was attacked by robbers. They stripped him of his clothes, beat him and went away, leaving him half dead.  31  A priest happened to be going down the same road, and when he saw the man, he passed by on the other side.  32  So too, a Levite, when he came to the place and saw him, passed by on the other side.  33  But a Samaritan, as he traveled, came where the man was; and when he saw him, he took pity on him.  34  He went to him and bandaged his wounds, pouring on oil and wine. Then he put the man on his own donkey, brought him to an inn and took care of him.  35  The next day he took out two denarii [c]  and gave them to the innkeeper. ‘Look after him,’ he said, ‘and when I return, I will reimburse you for any extra expense you may have.’ 36  “Which of these three do you think was a neighbor to the man who fell into the hands of robbers?” 37  The expert in the law replied, “The one who had mercy on him.” Jesus told him, “Go and do likewise.”
