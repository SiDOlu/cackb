FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: Remember Ye Not

TEXT: Isaiah 43:18-19

Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old. Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.  Beloved, we are enjoined by God to remember not the former things. Former things that represents your difficulties, pains, disappointments and those things that did not go the way you thought they should. The Lord says that is nothing to be compared to what He (God) is going to do. God promised us a brand new thing. God has fulfilled that promise to the Israelites and I prayed that He will do far more than we thought in our lives, families and Christ Apostolic Church Kingdom Builders as a whole in Jesus name. Stop thinking and talking about how big your mountain is and began to think and talk about how big your God is. Remain Blessed & Happy Sunday to you all.
