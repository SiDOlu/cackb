FOOD FOR SOUL

TOPIC: Pass The Salt

TEXT: Matthew 5:13

You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its saltiness, how can it be made salty again? It is no longer good for anything, except to be thrown out and trampled by men.
Beloved, let your influence seasoning people's life for the Kingdom. We are not to draw away from society, we are not of the world, but we live in the world and must rub our flavour on those who are perishing.
Salt is very necessary for life. Pure salt in Jesus' time was more valuable than gold because of what it is and what it does. Its seasoning causes flavour to come alive.
Christians are gift to life, society and their generations. Don't live a tasteless life, Be kind, merciful, peacemaker to the world around you.
Finally, Let your conversation be always full of grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how to answer everyone. Colossians 4:6. Pass on the salt that you are more than ever before.
Happy New Month to you all.
